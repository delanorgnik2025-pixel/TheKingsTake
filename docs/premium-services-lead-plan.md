## Automation to build next in the existing architecture

1. Approved ads or profile discovery lead to the relevant service page. Capture a bounded UTM source, campaign and landing page on the existing inquiry; do not store arbitrary sensitive query strings.
2. Preserve the inquiry in `site_leads` first. Classify by selected package, stated budget and timeline. Store qualification as guidance, not an automated rejection.
3. Show an acknowledgment and booking option. Configure a calendar link only after the owner chooses the booking service and availability.
4. Draft a tailored proposal from the actual request. Require owner review before scope/price commitments or sending. Separate project-response consent from marketing consent.
5. After an accepted scope, use the existing server-priced Stripe checkout and signed webhook confirmation. Count sales only on verified payment events, not visits or a success-page redirect.
6. If ongoing promotional follow-ups are desired, collect explicit marketing consent, add unsubscribe/stop handling and a deduplicated delivery queue. Stop on opt-out, booking, payment or owner pause.
7. Dashboard: spend, inquiries, qualified inquiries, bookings, signed scope, verified paid deposits; calculate cost per qualified lead and acquisition cost with attributed data. Set campaign caps and alerts before starting spend.

No guarantee of leads or sales. Start one controlled campaign, inspect qualified inquiries and payment results, then adjust audience/offer. Avoid simultaneous broad campaigns that obscure what works.
