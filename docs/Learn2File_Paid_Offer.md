# Learn2File — where the free course ends and the paid offer begins

The free product is a complete filing-foundation learning path: JS 44, fee/IFP, both application versions, checkpoints and a final test. Its outcome is understanding the packet, not a legal determination, lawsuit filed by us, or permission to represent others. Official court forms remain available free. Never make a deadline-critical instruction depend on purchasing a course.

The upgrade appears after completion, when the learner has experienced tangible progress. The live completion screen links to the owner's Facebook subscription page, book and next-course waitlist. It does not collect payment for unfinished lessons or claim automated subscriber verification.

## Proposed offer ladder — not products already launched

| Level | Deliverable | Charging point |
|---|---|---|
| Free foundation | Nine guided lessons and eight-question test; future reviewed video edition | Always free |
| Facebook community | Published subscriber benefits, course updates and moderated general educational discussion | Use the actual price and benefits configured in Ronald's Facebook dashboard; these have not been independently verified here |
| Casefile Lab | Completed branching fictional scenarios: record provenance, state action, identifying asserted rights, defendant-role research, complaint structure, court process and common procedural mistakes | Sell only once the promised lessons, access terms and server-side entitlement verification are ready |
| Book bundle | Existing book offer plus a clearly defined completed educational product | State formats, delivery dates, fulfillment and bundle inclusions accurately; do not imply the book itself guarantees a legal result |

Unique presentation: “The Passenger’s Casefile” is a phone-friendly mission sequence. A learner receives a fictional stop timeline and records, sorts fact from inference, identifies missing information, makes a research choice, sees the consequence and revisits it. The teacher is Ronald; the plaintiff Jordan is a separate fictional character. No simulated victory, legal credential, case diagnosis or individually tailored filings. The educational value is guided practice, clear explanations and feedback, not forms sold as secret law.

## Launch sequence

1. Publish the improved free course and test it with existing readers.
2. Produce one short Artlist pilot from the storyboard. Review likeness, narration, accessibility and legal accuracy before producing the rest.
3. Collect next-course interest through /buildyourcase. Measure real enrollments and voluntary feedback; local course progress is not a server-verified completion metric.
4. Finish the Casefile Lab content and review the relevant state rules governing the proposed educational business. If offering individualized feedback, keep it limited to educational exercises unless qualified counsel provides a separately governed service.
5. Confirm Facebook price/benefits and decide whether the course is included in a subscription or sold separately. Do not charge twice without a clear distinction.
6. Implement trusted paid-entitlement verification, renewal/revocation, terms and fulfillment before enabling paid lessons. Existing invitation codes or self-reported Facebook subscription flags do not prove ongoing payment.

No price, sales conversion or $100,000 monthly revenue is guaranteed. No course payments, paid entitlements, emails or generated videos were added by this release.
