# Learn2File — Artlist storyboard and scene prompts

Prepared October 14, 2026 for Ronald Lee King / AASOTU Media Group LLC / #TheKingsTake.

The guided website course is the source of truth: nine lessons and eight final questions. This pack stages the video edition; no video generation or credits have been used. These are model-agnostic prompts. Select a model supporting reference images and the required duration in your own Artlist account. Review one pilot before generating the batch.

## Character and production setup

Use a clear, current photograph of Ronald that he approves as the character reference. Use the same reference for every presenter scene. Preserve his facial features, skin tone, age, hair, facial hair and proportions. Do not invent a different likeness from this description. If the model supports only image-to-video, create one approved presenter still first, then animate that same frame. Reference support and dialogue accuracy depend on the selected model.

Ronald is the presenter, not the fictional plaintiff. Wardrobe: dark navy jacket, simple shirt, restrained gold accent; no judge robe, attorney badge, police uniform or claimed professional credential. Set: a premium editorial teaching studio, navy/black with warm gold desk light, bookshelves, muted nighttime city through a window. Calm, direct and approachable.

Shoot a 16:9 master with presenter centered and generous safe space for a 9:16 crop. For vertical-only production, select 9:16 and keep hands, face and key objects inside the central safe area. Generate short 6–8 second visual clips, reuse the presenter setup, and edit into 60–120 second lessons with screen recordings and narration. Do not generate an entire detailed legal lesson in one clip.

Use the real court PDFs/screen recordings for readable form text; generated forms are not reliable. Add all captions, dollar amounts, citations, labels, branding and disclosures in the editor after generation. Use Ronald's own recorded narration for authenticity; no voice cloning is needed. Keep music low under speech, use your licensed Artlist track, and retain the applicable license record.

## Editing and publishing checklist

- Confirm the presenter likeness before generating all scenes.
- Verify narration against the live course and current court sources. Check pronunciation: in FOR-ma POW-per-is; spell out I-F-P once.
- Label every Jordan shot “Fictional scenario.” Do not imply it documents Ronald's own litigation.
- Composite genuine JS 44/AO 239/AO 240 forms. Use invented practice data; never upload real financial records or identifying information to the generator.
- Add accurate captions and transcripts. Avoid rapid flashing cuts. Render legible phone-size form close-ups.
- Keep the free-course CTA truthful. Facebook subscription does not automatically issue a website code or unlock an unfinished paid course.
- Produce one pilot for approval, then batches of reviewed clips. No auto-generation schedule.
